Research Interests

My work sits at the intersection of structural biology and data science, with a focus on turning experimental interaction data into accessible, well-annotated resources.

Protein–Peptide Interactions

Characterising binding interfaces, affinity determinants and recognition motifs that govern how peptides engage protein targets.

Binding sitesMotif discoveryDocking

Structural Bioinformatics

Analysing 3D structures and predicted models to understand conformational change, stability and function at the residue level.

PDB analysisMolecular dynamicsAlphaFold

Biological Databases

Designing curated, searchable databases and web platforms that make interaction data findable, accessible, interoperable and reusable (FAIR).

Data curationMySQLWeb platforms

Machine Learning for Biology

Building predictive models for interaction propensity and binding affinity from sequence and structural features.

PythonFeature engineeringDeep learning

Current focus

Building an integrated research platform that links curated protein–peptide interaction records with structural models and predictive scores, so researchers can move from a query to a testable hypothesis in one place.
