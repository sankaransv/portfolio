Research Interests

Computational approaches to understanding how proteins recognise peptides — from curated data to structure to prediction

My research sits at the intersection of structural biology and data science. I build resources and methods that connect experimental interaction data with 3D structure, so that binding can be explained, compared and predicted at scale.

Research Themes

01

Protein–Peptide Interactions

Characterising binding interfaces, affinity determinants and recognition motifs that govern how short peptides engage protein targets.

Binding sitesMotif discoveryDocking
02

Structural Bioinformatics

Analysing experimental and predicted 3D structures to understand conformational change, stability and function at the residue level.

PDB analysisMolecular dynamicsAlphaFold
03

Biological Databases

Designing curated, searchable databases and web platforms that make interaction data findable, accessible, interoperable and reusable (FAIR).

Data curationMySQLWeb development
04

Machine Learning for Biology

Building predictive models of interaction propensity and binding affinity from sequence and structural features.

PythonGraph neural networksFeature engineering
