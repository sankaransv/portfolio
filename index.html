About Me

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Biotechnology, Indian Institute of Technology Madras working with Prof. Michael Gromiha. My research combines structural bioinformatics, curated biological datasets and artificial intelligence (AI) to understand how peptides recognise and bind proteins.
