About Me

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Biotechnology, Indian Institute of Technology Madras, working with Prof. M. Michael Gromiha. My broader research interest lies in understanding biomolecular interactions at structural levels—how biological molecules recognize and interact with one another, and how structural, physicochemical, and energetic properties govern binding specificity and affinity. I am particularly interested in the structural basis of molecular recognition and in understanding how sequence variations and mutations influence molecular interactions, stability, and biological function. In my previous research, I studied peptide–membrane interactions, investigating how peptides belonging to different structural classes interact with biological membranes and how peptide sequence and structural characteristics influence their membrane association and interaction behavior.
For my PhD, I focus on understanding the molecular determinants of protein–peptide recognition and binding by integrating structural bioinformatics, and artificial intelligence. My research involves investigating peptide–protein binding affinity, sequence–structure–function relationships, and the structural determinants governing molecular recognition. I employ machine learning and deep learning approaches alongside structure-based computational methods to develop predictive models and computational tools for protein–peptide interaction analysis and rational peptide design.
